Taking notes while reading

Flip two cards and match each concept with its meaning. Every game uses 8 pairs from a deck of 16, so each round is different.

Students

Tries0

How to play

  1. All cards start face down.
  2. On your turn, flip two cards.
  3. If they are a concept and its meaning, you keep the pair and play again.
  4. If they do not match, they turn face down again and the next player goes.
  5. When all pairs are found, the player with the most pairs wins.
